Service 01

Optical bench
prototyping

Design and assembly of experimental optical benches integrating precision optics (aspheric lenses, mirrors, diffraction gratings), beam-steering elements and coherent or incoherent imaging systems.

Each subsystem is designed accounting for thermomechanical alignment constraints, vibration stability (passive/active isolation) and compatibility with associated metrology equipment (profilometer, Fizeau interferometer, beam analyser).

01
Optomechanical mount design
3, 4, 6-DOF mounts · Materials: Invar, Ti-6Al-4V, Al 7075 · Cryogenic compatibility on request
02
Beam steering and shaping
Afocal telescopes · Beam expanders · Collimation systems · Single-mode / PM fibre coupling
03
Integration and qualification
Wavefront sensing alignment (Shack-Hartmann) · WFE measurement · Qualification report provided
04
3D printing of optical structures
Mounts, baffles, diaphragms · Carbon-loaded opaque materials · SLA printing for smooth finish

Service 02

Optical simulation
& tolerancing

Full optical system modelling via ray tracing (sequential and non-sequential). Wavefront error budget (WFE budget) established through sensitivity analysis of mechanical, thermal and manufacturing parameters.

Monte-Carlo analysis on manufacturing tolerances (tilt, decentre, form errors), performance distribution calculations and definition of achievable production specifications.

SoftwareZemax OpticStudio · Code V · ANSYS Mechanical
Spectral rangesUV (193 nm) → LWIR (14 μm)
TolerancingRSS · Monte-Carlo · Inverse sensitivity
DeliverablesWFE report · Toleranced drawings · Optical datasheet
Modelled effectsBirefringence · Scatter · Absorption · Thermoelastic
StandardsISO 10110 · MIL-PRF-13830 · ECSS-E-HB-10-02A
Service 03

Photonic
MEMS

Design and consulting on micro-electromechanical systems (MEMS) with optical functionality — deformable mirrors, piezoelectric actuators, spatial light modulators (SLM) and integrated photodetectors. Mechanical–optical–electronic co-design approach for compact, high-performance architectures.

Deformable
mirrors

Design of segmented or continuous MEMS mirrors for adaptive wavefront correction. Electrostatic / piezo actuator simulation.

Actuator stroke: 1–50 μm
Bandwidth: up to 10 kHz
Substrates: SOI, SiN, PDMS
Spatial light
modulators

Integration and characterisation of SLM (DMD, LCOS) in phase or amplitude control architectures. Pixel-level calibration and non-uniformity compensation.

Phase accuracy: λ/100
Spatial resolution: < 8 μm/pixel
Compatible: Digital holography
Hermetic
packaging

Hermetic package design for photonic MEMS components. Fibre/component alignment management and anti-reflection optical windows.

Sealing: He-leak < 10⁻⁹ mbar·l/s
Temperature range: –55°C to +125°C
Standard: MIL-STD-883
